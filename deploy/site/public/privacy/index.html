Privacy, plainly.

Updated November 22, 2026. This page describes the public Roost website and the current, self-hosted Roost v3 software. Roost does not offer public managed-service signup.

This website

The marketing site does not include analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or third-party font requests. Its font, images, and scripts are served from this site. Copying the install command asks your browser to write that command to your clipboard; it does not read your clipboard. If you change the website’s theme, your browser remembers that preference locally.

OVH hosts the website. Cloudflare provides its network delivery and protection. Those providers may process IP addresses, request metadata, and security signals to deliver and protect the site, under their own policies.

Your self-hosted Roost

You operate your coordinator and worker machines. Your programs run on those workers. The coordinator handles session metadata, authorization, settings, and terminal traffic when a direct connection is unavailable. Your browser keeps a device key and local preferences so it can reconnect to your installation.

Running a program through Roost does not change that program’s own data practices. Coding assistants, optional dictation services, your chosen network provider, and other integrations may communicate with third parties when you configure or use them.

Your controls

You control the deployment’s logs, databases, retained terminal history, device authorization, and backups. Revoke devices you no longer trust and keep the host and front door secure. Clearing browser storage removes that browser’s local pairing; it does not delete records or backups on your host.

Questions

Read the setup guide, inspect the source code, or ask a question in the project’s issue tracker. Do not post private terminal content, security-sensitive details, or credentials in a public issue.
